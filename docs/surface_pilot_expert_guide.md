# Expert guide: supervised radiographic surface pilot

This study evaluates a workflow for assessing current radiographic alveolar bone loss. It does not ask you to make a definitive clinical diagnosis of periodontitis, and it does not estimate probing depth, clinical attachment loss, inflammation, or a guaranteed future outcome.

Use only your anonymous study ID. Do not enter names, patient identifiers, or other unnecessary personal information.

## Phase A — independent review

Model output is hidden. Confirm the arch, patient side, display orientation, and whether the displayed image is horizontally flipped. Mesial faces the dental midline; distal faces away. Never treat image-left or image-right as M/D without first verifying orientation and FDI identity.

For each visible tooth, confirm the FDI number and M/D pixel boundaries. Place the surface-specific CEJ at the radiographic cemento-enamel junction, the bone point at the alveolar-crest/tooth intersection, and the apex reference at the visible root apex. For multi-rooted teeth, select and identify the apex you judge appropriate; there is no automatic apex selection. Mark a landmark not visible when it cannot be located reliably. Mark the surface ungradable when the required anatomy or orientation cannot support a defensible measurement.

Save drafts as needed. Confirm and lock Phase A only when the independent review is complete. A locked Phase A record cannot be edited.

## Phase B — assisted comparison

After Phase A is locked, reveal the model snapshot. Compare it with your independent record, note disagreements, and record whether the suggestion is acceptable. Any assisted correction is stored separately and never changes Phase A or the model snapshot.

Warnings are review aids, not diagnoses. Report incorrect warnings or technical problems. The displayed RBL is a radiographic bone-loss percentage derived from confirmed landmarks and is decision support only.
